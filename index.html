How to play: move your hand to steer the paw · close your hand over a fruit and hold still for a moment to pick it up · keep your hand closed while you carry it to the matching basket · open your hand to drop it in. Drop a fruit in the wrong basket and it'll bounce back out — try again! 2 players: show both hands — each gets its own colored cursor and you sort at the same time. 3-5 players: everyone shares one hand and takes turns; swipe your hand quickly to the right, or tap "Next Player", to pass the turn. (You can also click-and-drag with a mouse to test.)
