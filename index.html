How to play: move your hand to steer the paw · close your hand over a fruit and hold still for a moment to pick it up · keep your hand closed while you carry it to the matching basket · open your hand to drop it in. Drop a fruit in the wrong basket and it'll bounce back out — try again! Everyone plays at the same time — each hand shown to the camera gets locked to its own colored cursor, even if hands cross paths. (You can also click-and-drag with a mouse to test.)
